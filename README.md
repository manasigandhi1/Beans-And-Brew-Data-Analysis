☕ Coffee Sales & Profitability Analysis
📌 Project Overview
Analyzed two years of coffee sales data to identify key drivers of revenue and profitability, and uncover reasons behind performance fluctuations.
The goal was to provide actionable insights to improve product strategy and business performance.

[![IMAGE ALT TEXT HERE](https://img.youtube.com/vi/wrYgyUhhbis/0.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wrYgyUhhbis)


🎯 Business Problem / Objective
The business experienced fluctuations in sales and profit over two years. 

This project answers:
  Which coffee types are driving or hurting profitability?
  How do roast types impact sales and margins?
  Why did performance decline in 2022?
  What product mix should be prioritized to improve profitability?
  
📊 Dataset Description
  Source: Internal coffee sales dataset
  Time Period: 2019–2022
  
Key Columns:
  Coffee Type (Arabica, Robusta, Liberica, Excelsa)
  Roast Type (Light, Medium, Dark)
  Sales, Profit, Quantity Sold
  Profit Margin %
  Monthly and yearly performance data
  
🛠 Tools & Technologies Used
  SQL → Data cleaning and transformation
  Power BI → Dashboard creation and visualization
  Excel → Initial exploration and validation
  
🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation
  Removed inconsistent and duplicate records
  Standardized coffee and roast type categories
  Handled missing values in sales and profit fields
  Created calculated metrics such as profit margin and YoY performance
  Structured data for analysis and dashboarding
  
🔍 Key Analysis & Insights
  Liberica is the most profitable coffee type with the highest margins
  Excelsa drives the highest sales volume with strong profitability
  Arabica shows moderate performance but lower margins
  Robusta is the weakest performer across all key metrics
  Light Roast contributes the highest sales and profit
  Dark Roast consistently underperforms
  Profit margins remained stable (~10%), indicating:
    Profit decline was not due to pricing or cost issues
    It was driven by reduced sales volume
  Significant drop in sales and profit observed in 2022
  Indicates a demand-side issue rather than margin erosion
  
📈 Dashboard / Visualization

An interactive Power BI dashboard was created to provide a complete view of business performance:
  KPI Cards → Quick overview of sales, profit, quantity, and margin
  Yearly Trends → Track changes in sales and profitability over time
  Coffee Type Analysis → Identify top and underperforming products
  Roast Type Analysis → Understand contribution to revenue and margins
  Scatter Plot → Compare volume, revenue, and profitability together
  Product-Level Insights → Highlight top-performing products
  Monthly Trends → Identify seasonal fluctuations
  Parameter Toggle → Switch between Sales, Profit, and Quantity

This dashboard enables stakeholders to quickly identify performance gaps and opportunities.

💡 Business Recommendations
  Scale Liberica production to maximize profit
  Use Excelsa to maintain and grow sales volume
  Improve pricing or positioning of Arabica to increase margins
  Reduce or phase out Robusta due to poor performance
  Focus on Light Roast in marketing and production
  Reassess strategy for Dark Roast products
  Optimize product mix toward high-margin combinations
  Investigate the 2022 demand drop and address root causes
  Run targeted promotions on top-performing products
  Reduce costs in low-margin segments

🚀 Impact / Value
This analysis helps the business:
  Identify high-profit and high-volume products
  Optimize product portfolio for better returns
  Make data-driven decisions on product strategy
  Understand performance decline and prevent future losses
  Improve overall profitability through better product mix
